About this prototype

gnARly is an interactive lab built around the XTI Passive Dynamic Robot Walker by Chance Robotics. Beta pilot audience: college engineering students (proposed: UWF Robotics & Intelligent Systems).

Physics model: planar compass-gait walker (straight legs, point masses, inelastic heel strike), solved in real time. The knee bend you see is visual only. The real XTI adds knees, elastic cord and side-to-side rocking; replace the placeholder geometry with the real STLs via tools/stl_to_glb.py and parts.json.

AR: On iPhone and iPad, “View in your space” opens Apple Quick Look with an animated USDZ of the walker stepping down a 3° ramp (no app install). On ARCore Android phones, Chrome can run the live physics in your room through WebXR.
